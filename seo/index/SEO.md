# ScaleFront Homepage SEO Strategy — Verified (DataForSEO, India)

All keyword data below is verified via DataForSEO (India location), not estimated. Score = keyword difficulty (0–100, lower is easier). Vol = monthly search volume.

---

## Primary Keyword Decision

**H1 / Title Tag / Meta Description:** `Hire Shopify Developer`

Changed from the earlier plan. Real data shows this outperforms `shopify development agency` on the metrics that matter:

| Keyword                      | Volume | Score | Intent   |
| ---------------------------- | ------ | ----- | -------- |
| `hire shopify developer`     | 720    | 28    | **Comm** |
| `shopify development agency` | 590    | 27    | Nav      |

Higher volume, similar difficulty, and — critically — classified as **commercial intent** (buying signal) vs. the agency term's **navigational intent** (lower conversion signal). It also matches your actual freelancer positioning honestly, which we flagged earlier as a trust/conversion factor, not just an SEO one.

**Local signal (subheading + schema, not H1):** Confirmed via data — `shopify development agency gurgaon` returned **no standalone search volume** (fell back to generic "Shopify" results). This validates the original plan: local relevance belongs in the subheading and LocalBusiness schema, not as a dedicated keyword target or H1.

## Key Findings From Verification

1. **Original volume estimates in the first draft were significantly overstated** (e.g. "shopify development agency" estimated at 5K–10K/month; actual India volume is 590).
2. **"Hire" + role phrasing outperforms "agency"/"company" phrasing** in both volume and commercial-intent classification — consistent finding across multiple searches.
3. **Exact-phrase local and comparison keywords have negligible standalone search volume.** Local relevance should be handled through schema/subheading, not keyword targeting. Comparison content ("vs agency", "how to vet") should be written for trust-building, not SEO traffic.
4. **Two genuine zero-difficulty opportunities exist**: `shopify development company in india` and `shopify website development cost in india` — both essentially uncontested right now. Prioritize these in early content.

# Industrial Supply Direct — Portfolio Page SEO Strategy

Verified via DataForSEO (India), OpenSEO Pro. Self-initiated/concept project — not a paid client engagement (see positioning note below).

---

## Keyword Verification Summary

Tested 5 phrase variations before finding the real target. Documenting the dead ends so this isn't re-tested later:

| Keyword tested                        | Result                                                | Verdict                        |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `shopify b2b development`             | No standalone volume — fell back to generic "Shopify" | Dead                           |
| `shopify wholesale store development` | No standalone volume — fell back to generic "Shopify" | Dead                           |
| `shopify quote request`               | 10 vol, Nav intent                                    | Dead                           |
| `shopify b2b demo store`              | 10 vol, Nav intent                                    | Dead                           |
| `shopify b2b store`                   | 10 vol, Comm intent                                   | Too low, use as secondary only |

**Lesson learned:** long-tail "service description" phrases (X + development, X + store) consistently return near-zero volume across this whole project. Short head terms carry the real, undiluted volume. Check the head term before assuming a service-description long-tail is dead.

## Verified Target Keywords

### Primary

| Keyword       | Volume | Score  | Intent |
| ------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| `shopify b2b` | 260    | **29** | Comm   |

Lowest difficulty of the four ~260-volume variants tested (`b2b on shopify` – 50, `shopify for b2b` – 46, `b2b shopify` – 50, `shopify b2b` – 29). Also the most natural phrase a real searcher would type.

### Secondary

| Keyword                 | Volume | Score | Intent |
| ----------------------- | ------ | ----- | ------ |
| `shopify b2b app`       | 50     | —     | Comm   |
| `shopify b2b commerce`  | 30     | —     | Comm   |
| `shopify b2b features`  | 30     | —     | Comm   |
| `shopify b2b ecommerce` | 30     | —     | Comm   |

Use as natural body-copy variation, not individual H2 targets — volume too low to justify dedicated sections.

### Explicitly excluded (low volume, wrong intent, or irrelevant)

- `shopify b2b login`, `shopify b2b portal`, `shopify b2b channel` — Navigational, people looking for Shopify's own product login, not a developer
- Everything else in the long-tail `shopify b2b [x]` cluster — 10 vol or less, mostly informational (Shopify's own documentation/pricing queries), not relevant to a dev/agency page
- `shopify b2b development`, `shopify wholesale store development`, `shopify quote request` — confirmed dead, do not re-target

## Page Structure & On-Page SEO

**Title tag:** `Industrial Supply Direct — Shopify B2B Development Case Study | ScaleFront`

**Meta description:** One honest sentence, includes `shopify b2b` naturally, no fabricated claims.

**H1:** `Industrial Supply Direct` (project name — case study convention)
**H2 subhead directly under H1:** works `shopify b2b` naturally, e.g. "A self-initiated Shopify B2B build for technical, catalog-heavy buyers."

**Body sections (H2s):**

1. The problem this explores (sets up B2B pain point — no forced keyword here, natural framing)
2. Structuring the catalog for technical buyers (`shopify b2b commerce`, `shopify b2b features` fit naturally)
3. Building a real quote request flow (functional description, no forced keyword — `shopify quote request` confirmed dead, don't force it)
4. Component cross-selling / AOV design intent (framed as design hypothesis, not claimed result — per site-wide "no unverified stats" rule)

**Image alt text:** Every screenshot gets unique, descriptive alt text referencing the vertical, e.g. `"Industrial Supply Direct B2B product page with tabbed technical specifications"`.

**Internal linking:**

- Link to `/services/shopify-b2b-development` (build this service page next — same keyword target, commercial-intent page, reinforces topical relevance both ways)
- Link back to homepage and to Featured Work index

## Positioning & Content Integrity Rules (carried over from site-wide standards)

1. **No fabricated metrics.** No invented conversion %, no invented client names/testimonials. This is self-initiated work — frame outcomes as design intent ("designed to reduce back-and-forth before a sales call"), not claimed results ("reduced X by Y%").
2. **No implied client relationship.** Copy should read as build/creator voice ("we built," "this project explores") — never "the client needed" or similar phrasing that implies a commercial engagement that didn't happen.
3. **"We" language**, consistent with homepage positioning decision — not "I," to avoid solo-developer perception risk, but also not overselling team size elsewhere on the page.
4. **No shared boilerplate** with the other 7 portfolio pages — this page's copy must be structurally and substantively distinct (this is naturally satisfied here since B2B is the most different vertical in the portfolio, by design).

## Schema

Use `CreativeWork` or `Article` structured data. Do not use `Review` or `Product` schema — there's no real transaction or customer review to back it.

## Decision: No Dedicated Primary Keyword — Authority & Technical Credibility Page Only

Similar to Iron Crate and Industrial Supply Direct, high-volume terms like `shopify upsell app` (590 IN / 1,900 UK) and `shopify cross sell app` (210 IN / 720 UK) were deliberately rejected on **search intent grounds**:

- **Navigational & App-Store Intent:** Users searching for "app" keywords want an instant 1-click plugin installation from the Shopify App Store, not a technical deep-dive into custom theme architecture.
- **Topical Alignment:** A portfolio case study analyzing bespoke Liquid 2.0 components, native "Buy It With" bundling, and a custom tiered-reward cart drawer cannot satisfy app-store download intent.
- **Consistent India-First Positioning:** Optimizing for UK/US app queries would conflict with the site-wide domestic positioning established across service pages, schema data, and contact architecture.

**Conclusion:** This page serves as an authoritative technical demonstration of high-ticket eCommerce CRO, native cross-selling, and clinical trust engineering. Its primary SEO purpose is to reinforce internal linking authority toward `/services/theme-development` and `/services/conversion-optimization`.

## Positioning & Content Integrity Rules

1. **Concept / Exploration Framing:** Present RecovrX as a self-initiated design and engineering build ("we built", "this project explores"). Do not fabricate a client relationship or pretend to be an agency of record for a legacy enterprise.
2. **No Fabricated Commercial Results:** Do not claim unverified conversion percentage increases (e.g., "boosted revenue by 48%"). Frame all mechanics as **design intent, UX hypotheses, and architectural solutions** (e.g., "engineered to reduce purchasing friction for high-ticket equipment").
3. **Voice Consistency:** Use "we" phrasing representing ScaleFront's engineering practice. Avoid solo "I" and avoid implying a bloated agency team.
4. **Distinct Technical Storytelling:** Differentiate RecovrX from Iron Crate and Industrial Supply Direct by focusing on **diagnostic health/recovery funnel routing**, **native 4-item 'Buy It With' bundling**, and **clinical lab trial presentation**.

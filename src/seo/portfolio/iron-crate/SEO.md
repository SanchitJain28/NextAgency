## Decision: No Dedicated Primary Keyword — Portfolio/Authority Page Only

Two real, decent-volume candidates exist (`shopify bundle app` – 390 IN, `shopify subscription app` – 480 IN / 2,400 UK), but both were rejected on **intent grounds**, not volume grounds:

- Both are **Navigational/Transactional intent** — searchers want to _find and install_ an existing app, not _hire a developer_ to build custom bundle/subscription functionality.
- A portfolio case study cannot satisfy that search intent even if it ranked — the searcher's need and the page's content don't match. This repeats a lesson from earlier pages on this site: real search volume doesn't make a keyword a good target if intent doesn't align with what the page delivers (same conclusion reached on `shopify quote request` and `shopify plus migration`).

**UK-market targeting was also explicitly considered and rejected** — optimizing this single page for UK searchers would conflict with the site's consistent India-first positioning (established across the homepage, local schema, pricing FAQ, and every other keyword decision in this project). Pursuing UK traffic on one isolated portfolio page without a broader business decision to target international clients was judged not worth the positioning inconsistency.

**Conclusion:** This page is built for portfolio depth, technical credibility, and internal-linking support — not as a standalone SEO traffic target. This mirrors the same conclusion reached for the Industrial Supply Direct (B2B) page.

## Page Structure & On-Page SEO

**Title tag:** `Iron Crate — Custom Shopify Bundle Builder & Cart Case Study | ScaleFront`
(Descriptive, not keyword-optimized for ranking — written for clarity and internal-link anchor relevance instead.)

**Meta description:** One honest sentence describing the actual build — bundle architecture, tiered-reward cart, subscription toggle. No fabricated claims.

**H1:** `Iron Crate` (project name — case study convention, consistent with other portfolio pages)

**Body sections (H2s), based on the real architecture breakdown:**

1. The problem this explores (supplement-brand AOV/retention challenges — natural framing, no forced keyword)
2. Homepage & discovery architecture (category matrix, trust/certification grid — descriptive, not keyword-driven)
3. Product detail page architecture (dosage summary, subscription toggle — mention `shopify bundle builder` naturally if relevant)
4. The Build Your Own Stack bundle system (`/build-your-own`) — natural home for `shopify bundle builder` mention
5. The tiered-reward cart (gamified spend milestones — framed as design intent/hypothesis, not claimed AOV result, per site-wide "no unverified stats" rule)

**Image alt text:** Unique per screenshot, e.g. `"Iron Crate build-your-own supplement stack bundle builder interface"`.

**Internal linking:**

- Link to `/services/shopify-theme-development` (verified real keyword, Score 8, IN — carries actual SEO weight; this page inherits relevance via the link rather than targeting its own keyword)
- Link back to homepage and Featured Work index
- This page's internal-link role matters more than its own ranking potential — treat outbound links from here as reinforcing the service page's topical authority, not the other way around

## Positioning & Content Integrity Rules (carried over from site-wide standards)

1. **No fabricated metrics.** The "55+ years bodybuilding pedigree," "Olympians and NBA programs," and specific dosage/certification claims read as brand copy for a concept brand — rewrite or clearly frame as illustrative/concept content, not implied real credentials. Do not present invented brand history as fact.
2. **No implied client relationship.** Build/creator voice throughout ("we built," "this project explores") — never "the client needed."
3. **"We" language**, consistent with site-wide positioning — not "I," and not oversized team framing.
4. **No shared boilerplate** with the other 7 portfolio pages. This is naturally low-risk here given the project's genuinely distinctive features (bundle builder, tiered cart, dosage/clinical PDP structure) — but still cross-check against Iron Crate's own internal sections (homepage, PDP, and cart descriptions each risk repeating "gamified," "tiered-reward," "AOV" language internally — vary phrasing within the page itself, not just across pages).
5. **Frame all outcome language as design intent, not claimed results** — e.g. "the tiered progress bar is designed to encourage larger cart values," never "increased AOV by X%," since no real measurement exists for a self-initiated build.

## 3. Page Structure & On-Page Content Strategy

### Section 1: Hero

- **H1:** `Shopify Migration Service Built for Zero Downtime and Protected Search Rankings`
- **Subheading:** Plain language value proposition. Emphasize complete catalog, customer, and historical order transfer without transactional pauses.
- **CTAs:** "Book a Migration Audit" and "Discuss on WhatsApp".
- **Rules:** One clear headline, one concise paragraph, two direct action buttons. No unverified stat badges.

### Section 2: Traffic & SEO Preservation (Target: `shopify migration seo`)

- **Focus:** Directly address the merchant's primary fear: losing Google rankings and organic revenue post-migration.
- **Technical Details:**
  - Complete 1:1 URL crawl and automated 301 redirect map creation.
  - Meta titles, descriptions, and structured data carryover.
  - Preserving canonical hierarchies and image alt tags.
  - Google Search Console monitoring and 404 catch pipelines.

### Section 3: Platform-Specific Migration Paths (H2s)

1. **WooCommerce / WordPress to Shopify (`woocommerce to shopify migration`, `wordpress to shopify migration`)**:
   - Resolving database bloat, plugin overload, and slow server response times.
   - Mapping ACF (Advanced Custom Fields) and custom database tables into native Shopify Metafields and Metaobjects.
   - Converting WordPress blog content and media libraries into Shopify Online Store 2.0 blogs.
2. **Magento (Adobe Commerce) to Shopify (`magento to shopify migration services`)**:
   - Handling heavy product catalogs (10,000+ SKUs) and complex variant matrices.
   - Rebuilding custom B2B pricing rules, tiered pricing, and company account workflows.
   - Migrating away from dedicated server maintenance costs to Shopify's managed infrastructure.
3. **Shopify 2.0 & Store Re-Architecture (`shopify 2.0 migration`, `shopify to shopify migration`)**:
   - Upgrading legacy vintage liquid themes to modern JSON templates.
   - Consolidating multiple regional stores or transitioning wholesale portals.

### Section 4: What Data Gets Migrated (Data Integrity Matrix)

Clean, explicit breakdown of transferable assets:

- **Product Catalog:** Titles, descriptions, images, variant options, inventory levels, SKUs, barcodes, and tags.
- **Customer Data:** Name, contact details, shipping/billing addresses. _(Clear explanation that customer passwords cannot be exported due to industry-standard one-way encryption hashing; automated account activation email workflows are implemented instead)._
- **Order History:** Complete historical orders, line items, fulfillment status, and order timestamps.
- **Collections & Navigation:** Hierarchy mapping, category descriptions, and menu trees.
- **CMS & Content:** Pages, legal policies, blog posts, and author credits.

### Section 5: The 5-Phase Technical Migration Process

1. **Catalog Audit & Architecture Plan:** Reviewing current database structure, identifying required custom fields, and auditing third-party app dependencies.
2. **Staging Environment Setup:** Deploying an isolated development store; testing schema imports on a representative batch of products and customer accounts.
3. **Theme & Feature Rebuilding:** Rebuilding storefront layouts, custom logic, and checkout integrations using native Shopify features instead of app bloat.
4. **Pre-Launch Delta Sync:** Performing a final incremental sync of all new orders and customer registrations made on the old store during development.
5. **Zero-Downtime DNS Cutover:** Repointing domain records, running post-launch 301 redirect verification, and monitoring live transactions.

### Section 6: FAQs (30–35 Words Each, Direct & Plain English)

1. **Will my online store go offline during the migration process?**  
   No. Your existing store remains fully operational and continues taking orders throughout development. The final cutover occurs via DNS updates in minutes after all data has been verified.
2. **Will moving to Shopify cause my store to lose search engine rankings?**  
   Not when executed properly. We map every legacy URL to its new Shopify equivalent using strict 301 redirects, preserving historical page equity, metadata, and organic Google visibility.
3. **Can you migrate customer passwords from our old platform?**  
   No platform can export plaintext passwords due to secure encryption hashing. We migrate all customer profiles and setup automated activation emails so shoppers can reset passwords smoothly.
4. **How long does a complete store migration typically take?**  
   Most standard migrations take 2 to 4 weeks. This covers catalog data extraction, custom Liquid theme configuration, payment setup, comprehensive checkout testing, and final delta data synchronization.
5. **Why hire an independent developer rather than a traditional migration agency?**  
   You communicate directly with the developer executing your database scripts and theme code. This eliminates account manager overhead, prevents technical misunderstandings, and delivers faster turnarounds at fixed rates.

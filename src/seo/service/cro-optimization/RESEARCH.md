## Research - CRO optimization page

1. The systematic process of improving a website or app to increase the percentage of visitors who take a desired action

2. Formula: (Conversions ÷ Total Visitors) × 100
   Example: If 10,000 visitors come to your site and 200 make a purchase or sign up, your conversion rate is 2%

3. Key stratgies for CRO optimization

- A/B Testing : Which version of the page performs better or converts better
- Improve User Experience (UX): Speed up page load times, simplify navigation, and make mobile layouts seamless.
- Clear CTA : Use distinct, compelling buttons and text that tell users what to do next.
- Build Trust: Add social proof like customer reviews, testimonials, and trust badges.
- Reduce Friction: Shorten web forms and simplify the checkout process to stop users from leaving.

4. What is CRO campaingn ?

- A CRO campaign involves analyzing your current website experience, running tests, and personalizing experiences. If the changes positively impact KPIs, you make them permanent.

5. Landing Page design

- Landing page design is crucial for a website’s usability and success. A well-designed site attracts more visitors.
- Take Amazon’s product pages as an example. Most customers visit with the intent to purchase. Amazon’s design maximizes conversions by making key elements easy to find. For instance, the “Add to Cart” button is prominently displayed in bright orange next to the product details. This color stands out against the white background, drawing attention and prompting action.

6. Website structure

- Headline : Headline
  Your headline is the first thing website visitors see. It shapes their first impression and determines whether they stay. To make it effective:
  Writing style:
  - Ask a question: e.g., “Did you know email marketing can increase your revenue by 30%?”
  - Split content into parts: e.g., “Internet Marketing: What’s in store?”
  - Address directly: e.g., “Can you rely on content marketing?”
  - Focus on numbers: e.g., “10 ways to boost your conversions with email marketing.” Keep it concise and - clear, - focusing on what your product or service offers.

- Body content
  Your body content answers, “What’s in it for me?” It should be clear, concise, and represent your brand well. To craft compelling body content:

- Call to action
  A call-to-action (CTA) asks customers to take a specific action, like subscribing to a newsletter, booking a webinar, or making a purchase. The clearer and more compelling your CTA, the more leads it generates.

- Forms
  Forms are key to most businesses, especially in the sales funnel. Optimizing them can significantly boost conversions. While there are many theories about creating effective forms, what works depends on your business. Sometimes, a comprehensive form is needed, while in other cases, a simple form works better. The key is balancing lead quality and volume for the best ROI.

Here are three tips to optimize your forms:

User-friendly design: Clear, consistent styling, tooltips, and validation improve user experience. Prioritize important fields and experiment with progressive forms.
Simplified password creation: Help users create easy-to-remember passwords to speed up the form-filling process.
One-click submissions: Allowing one-click sign-ups via Facebook or Google makes conversions easier, especially if users are already logged in. However, this might not work for all businesses, especially B2B.

- Page speed directly impact the conversion rate,user experience and bounce rate , if the page loads slowly users are likely to get off the website

7. CRO Optimization techinques

- The research phase – Identifying the areas of improvement : Analyze quantitative data like bounce rates, traffic sources, demographics, and more on your website.

- The hypothesis phase :
  Remember, a logical hypothesis has three parts:

- A specific change based on behavioral research
- A desired effect (a conversion metric, for example)
- The reasoning behind the change.
- The A/B Testing phase – Testing different hypotheses : Run A/B tests to validate your hypotheses. Use tools like Google Optimize, Optimizely, or VWO to create variations of your website and measure their performance against the original.

- The prioritization phase –

`THE P.I.E RULE`

1. Potiential : Find pages that are worst performing and can improve gereatly
2. Importance : narrow down by selecting the ones that have most valuable traffic .
3. Ease : It’s important to go for the one that is easily optimized first and then move up the list.

- The testing phase - Whether you will use A/B testing , split or multivariate

`A/B Testing`

- You test two versions of something.
  If B gets significantly more conversions, you have evidence that B is better.

A/B = compare A vs B

`Split Testing`

- In practice, split testing usually means the same basic idea as A/B testing: split traffic between different versions.

`Multivariate testing`
This is different.

Instead of testing one major variation against another, you change multiple elements simultaneously and test their combinations.

- The learning phase – how to analyze A/B test results
  This phase is where you analyze your test results, complete the testing loop, and gather insights for future tests. Don’t be done with checking if a variation won or failed.

8. CRO for an eCommerce business

- Shopping cart abandonment is the biggest problem for ecommerce stores , costing billions in lost revenue each year. According to the Baymard Institute, 69% of eCommerce visitors abandon their carts for various reasons.

`Common Reasons`

- Distractions: Too many pop-ups or forms.
- Difficult checkout: Complicated checkout for first-time users.
- Hidden costs: Unexpected charges like shipping and taxes.

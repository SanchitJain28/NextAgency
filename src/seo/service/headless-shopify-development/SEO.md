## Decision: No Dedicated Primary Keyword — Authority/Technical Page Only

This is a clean, unambiguous dead end — unlike the theme-development or app-development pages (where a correctly-intentioned low-volume keyword was eventually found), **every angle tested for headless Shopify returns either negligible volume or Navigational intent.** People searching headless-Shopify terms are developers and technical researchers looking for documentation, examples, or discussion — not merchants evaluating whether to hire someone.

This matches the same conclusion reached on the three portfolio pages (Iron Crate, Industrial Supply Direct, FlexForm Athletics): **build for genuine value and internal linking, not for standalone search traffic.**

**What this page is for instead:**

- Establishing technical credibility on a genuinely complex, high-value service (headless builds are expensive, technically demanding work — even without search volume, this page matters for closing deals once a prospect is already talking to you)
- Internal linking target from `/services/shopify-theme-development` and `/services/custom-shopify-app-development`, where the site's real keyword weight lives, and from relevant portfolio pages if any headless work is showcased
- A page to send directly to prospects who ask about headless/Hydrogen/Next.js during a sales conversation — its value is conversion support, not organic discovery

## Page Structure & On-Page SEO

**Title tag:** `Headless Shopify Development | ScaleFront`
**Meta description:** One honest sentence describing the service — no keyword-optimization pressure since there's nothing real to target; write for clarity instead.
**H1:** Clear, plain-language headline. No forced keyword insertion.

**Body sections:** Follow the honest-tradeoff pattern established in the content-style skill (Section 3.C) — headless requires meaningfully higher upfront investment and ongoing maintenance, and for most stores a well-built custom Liquid theme delivers the same results at much lower cost. State this plainly, don't oversell headless as the default recommendation.

**Internal linking:**

- Link from `/services/shopify-theme-development` and `/services/custom-shopify-app-development` (both carry real keyword weight)
- Link to relevant portfolio work if any headless/Hydrogen build exists in the portfolio
- No FAQ keyword-targeting needed — write FAQ content for genuine buyer education (e.g. "do I need headless or will a custom theme do?") rather than chasing search volume

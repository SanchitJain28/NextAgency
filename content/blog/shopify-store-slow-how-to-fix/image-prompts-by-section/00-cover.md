Create a static editorial infographic for a practical Shopify developer blog. White background, near-black text, gray borders, restrained orange accents, clean sans-serif typography, generous spacing, clear arrows. Flat diagram style, no 3D mockups. Use only the supplied labels and facts; no invented analytics, clients, before/after results, rankings, or testimonials. Keep text large enough to read on mobile. Generate a still image, not a video. Title: “Why is your Shopify store slow?” Show three diagnostic branches labeled Loading (LCP), Interactions (INP), Layout shifts (CLS). Feed all branches into “Test a targeted fix” then “Verify shopping flows”. No numerical claims or invented speedometer scores.

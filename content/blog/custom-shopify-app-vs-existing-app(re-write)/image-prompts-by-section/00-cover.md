# Cover

Dimensions: 1600 × 900 px (16:9).

Placement: Blog cover and header.

Suggested alt text: Comparison of Shopify built-in features, an App Store app, and a custom app by the type of work each suits.

## Google Flow prompt

Use a white background, near-black text, thin gray outlines, restrained orange accents, and flat illustrations. Create a still image with clear spacing and readable type. Use the supplied labels and short explanations.

Create a wide editorial cover titled “Custom Shopify App vs Existing App”. Add the subtitle “Choose based on the work your store needs done.” Below, illustrate these three choices side by side:

- “Built-in Shopify features” / “Check whether your theme or settings already solve it.”
- “Existing App Store app” / “Use a supported app for a common requirement.”
- “Custom Shopify app” / “Build around a specific workflow or integration.”

Use a theme editor, an app tile, and connected system blocks as the illustrations. Give the existing and custom options equal visual weight, with the built-in option clearly marked “Check first”. Keep the title and main illustrations inside the center of the canvas so they stay readable in a blog card.

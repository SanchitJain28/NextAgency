# Cover

Dimensions: 1600 × 900 px (16:9).

Placement: Blog cover and header.

Suggested alt text: A Next.js storefront connected to a WooCommerce backend, with the storefront and store management roles shown side by side.

## Google Flow prompt

Create a still editorial cover for a technical ecommerce blog. Use a white background, near-black text, thin gray outlines, restrained orange accents, and flat illustrations. Use the supplied headings and supporting copy with readable type and clear spacing.

Create a wide editorial cover with an illustrated product page on the left and a store management panel on the right. Link the two with a clearly labeled API connection. Use the title “Headless WooCommerce with Next.js” and the subtitle “Architecture, setup, and migration”. Place these labels and supporting lines next to the matching illustrations:

- “Next.js storefront” / “Customers browse products and shop here.”
- “WooCommerce backend” / “Manage products, inventory, and orders in WordPress.”
- “API connection” on the link between the two sides.

Use the visual space to distinguish the shopper experience from store management. Leave room around the title for the blog card crop.

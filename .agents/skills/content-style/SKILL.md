# Clean, Plain English Content Style Guide

This skill defines the voice, vocabulary, and writing style for all ScaleFront content. It ensures that every page reads cleanly, naturally, and convincingly in everyday English without inflated marketing buzzwords or overly complex phrasing.

---

## 1. Core Writing Principles

1. **Speak like a practical builder**: Explain things the way an experienced developer explains them to a business owner over coffee.
2. **Clarity beats complexity**: If a sentence sounds complicated, break it down. If a word sounds pretentious, replace it with an everyday word.
3. **Concrete examples over abstract concepts**: Never make a vague claim when you can explain a simple real-world scenario.
4. **Short, direct sentences**: State the fact clearly, give the practical context, and move on.

---

## 2. Jargon Blacklist & Plain English Replacements

Avoid inflated vocabulary, pretentious descriptors, and artificial agency buzzwords. These words are banned because they're harder to understand — they exist to make content sound fancy, not to communicate.

| Don't use                       | Use instead                                  |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Bespoke                         | Custom                                       |
| Seamlessly integrates           | Connects to / works with                     |
| Robust architecture             | Built to handle [specific thing]             |
| Unlock your potential           | (state the actual outcome)                   |
| Holistic digital transformation | (name the specific change)                   |
| Operational model               | How your store runs                          |
| Leverage                        | Use                                          |
| Cutting-edge / state-of-the-art | (name the specific technology)               |
| Ecosystem                       | (name the actual tools/systems)              |
| Scalable                        | Handles more traffic/orders without breaking |
| Streamline                      | Remove steps / make faster                   |
| Empower                         | Let you / give you the ability to            |
| Best-in-class                   | (state the actual result or comparison)      |
| Synergy                         | (delete — rarely means anything)             |

If a word could be swapped into any other agency's website with zero changes, it's too vague. Replace it with the specific thing being built or the specific result the merchant will see.

## 3. Layout & Structure Rules

### A. Format Flow: Heading &rarr; Direct Content

- Start with a clear, descriptive H2 or H3 heading.
- Follow immediately with high-density, readable paragraphs.
- Avoid unnecessary boxed border cards or artificial number badges (`01`, `02`, `03`) unless displaying distinct case study media.

### B. Use Relatable Store Examples

Ground every technical capability in everyday eCommerce situations. Don't describe abstract concepts — describe what a real store owner would see or do.

#### Examples (Bad vs Good):

- ❌ "We engineer bespoke Liquid architectures that unlock your brand's true potential."
- ✅ "We write custom Liquid code so your product pages match exactly what you sell — not a generic template."

- ❌ "Our holistic digital transformation seamlessly integrates cutting-edge paradigm shifts into your operational model."
- ✅ "We connect your Shopify store to your warehouse system so inventory updates automatically when an order ships."

- ❌ "Leveraging modular schema mutations for dynamic line-item fee calculations across personalization touchpoints."
- ✅ "Customers pick 3 items for a gift box, the price updates on the page, and all 3 items add to the cart in one click."

- ❌ "Post-deployment, merchants gain autonomous content sovereignty through native CMS extensibility layers."
- ✅ "After launch, you can edit text, swap photos, and reorder sections in the Shopify admin without touching code."

### C. Honest Tradeoffs Build Trust

- Never oversell a complex technology.
- When explaining **Headless Shopify**, state honestly that it requires higher upfront investment and ongoing developer maintenance. Clarify that for 90% of stores, a well-built custom Liquid 2.0 theme delivers the same speed and results at a much lower cost.

### D. Image Layout Pattern (Alternating 2-Column Grids)

- Do NOT insert massive full-width screenshot blocks that occupy the entire screen height.
- Use an alternating 2-column grid layout on desktop:
  - **Section A**: Text and capability list on the Left (`lg:col-span-7`), Project screenshot on the Right (`lg:col-span-5`).
  - **Section B**: Project screenshot on the Left (`lg:col-span-5`), Text and capability list on the Right (`lg:col-span-7`).
- Keep images styled with clean borders (`border-2 border-[var(--sf-ink)] shadow-[4px_4px_0_var(--sf-ink)]`) and a concise caption bar underneath.

## 4. Headlines & CTAs

- Headlines state a concrete outcome or fact, not a mood ("Your store loads in under 2 seconds" not "Blazing-fast performance").
- CTAs name the action and, where possible, the result ("See a sample rebuild" not "Unlock your journey").
- Never use exclamation points to manufacture excitement.

---

## 5. FAQ Writing Rules & Strict Word Limits

- **Answer Length**: Every FAQ answer must be concise, direct, and strictly **30 to 35 words**.
- **No Preamble**: Answer the question in the very first sentence. Avoid introductory filler like _"That is a great question..."_ or _"When considering this aspect..."_.
- **Plain English**: Use short words and clear outcomes.

### Good FAQ Example:

> **Question:** Will custom development slow down my store?  
> **Answer:** No. Custom development is usually much faster than pre-made setups. We write clean Liquid and lightweight code, replacing 10 to 15 third-party apps that usually drag down mobile load speeds. _(32 words)_

---

## 6. Pre-Publication Checklist

Before finalizing any page copy, verify:

1. [ ] **No Blacklisted Words**: Check every word against the Section 2 table. Replace anything vague with the specific thing or outcome.
2. [ ] **Simple Examples**: Are examples easy to understand (e.g. 3-item box builders, ERP sync, drag-and-drop editing)?
3. [ ] **Headings &rarr; Content**: Is the layout a clean editorial reading flow without unnecessary cards?
4. [ ] **Headlines & CTAs**: Do they state a fact or action, not a feeling?
5. [ ] **FAQ Word Count**: Are all FAQ answers strictly between 30 and 35 words?
6. [ ] **Read-Aloud Test**: Does the copy sound like a friendly, experienced human developer, or does it sound like AI/marketing fluff?

## 7. Examples: Bad vs Good

- ❌ "Our robust architecture ensures seamless scalability across your operational model."
- ✅ "Your store can handle a 10,000-visitor flash sale without crashing, because we test load before launch."

- ❌ "We unlock your brand's true potential through bespoke digital experiences."
- ✅ "We build the site around how you actually sell — same-day shipping badges, size charts, whatever your customers ask about most."

- ❌ "Dynamic personalization touchpoints drive holistic engagement across the customer journey."
- ✅ "Returning customers see 'Reorder your usual' on the homepage instead of the same generic banner everyone else sees."

- ❌ "Our team leverages cutting-edge paradigms for enterprise-grade content sovereignty."
- ✅ "You can log into Shopify admin and change the homepage banner yourself — no developer needed."

- ❌ "We architect scalable fulfillment orchestration layers for omnichannel commerce."
- ✅ "When someone orders online, it shows up in your warehouse system automatically — no one retypes it."

- ❌ "Seamlessly integrated checkout experiences reduce friction across the conversion funnel."
- ✅ "We removed 4 steps from checkout. Customers now go from cart to 'order confirmed' in under 30 seconds."

# When should a store NOT migrate from WooCommerce to Shopify?

## When to migrate from woocommerce to shopify ?

- If you are looking to migrate from woocommerce to shopify , you must check these things before migrating

1. is your store catalog becoming large and complex ?

- If your store is slowing down or having high load times , this is a sign that you should transfer to Shopify because Woocommerce stores products , categories and other ecommerce objects in the form of wordpress posts , which are not natively made to handle large number of products or complex functionalities hence it can slow down your store if you have complex products schemmas or large number number of products

2. If you need a better UI / UX of your ecommerce store ?

- Woocommrece does not give much options to customize your UI and UX of the store , as woocommerce provide prebuild code blocks which you can directly install in your theme , which is not much flexible . In Shopify you can make a dedicated custom design of your store as shopify depends upon liquid template language which gives you full flexibilty to customize your store UI and UX.

## How to migrate

- Woocommerce to shopify migration have multiple steps which include data like products , collections , media assets , customer data and financial data such as order history , if you want to know more about the migration process please read our previous guide on how to migrate from woocommerce to shopify .

## When not to migrate to Shopify ?

- merchants offently does not need to migrate from woocommerce to shopify if handled correctly , but these are the conditions that tells you that you should not migrate to shopify

1. If you have a relatively small and simple products , woocommerce can easily handle this .

2. If your ecommerce site is content heavy means your website has a lot of blogs and content , then woocommerce is suitable for this because wordpress was natively built for content heavy websites hence wordpress / woocommrece are suitbale for these type of ecommerce websites

3. When your ecommerce site does not have much traffic then woocommerce is best for this usecase, because shopify and woocommerce performs similarly under low trafffic load .

## Problems faced during migration process ?

- Woocommerce to shopify migration process is not that easy as you think , these are the problems you can face during the migration process

1.  Complex product schemeas : If your woocommerce product has too many properites and metadata , it can be quite complex to migrate.

2.  Customer data migration : Woocommerce does not allow to migrate custom passwords directly to shopify due to secuirty reasons .

3.  Transactional and financial data migration : Shopify does not give a option to import these data , hence we need to use Shopify GraphQL API to migrate transactional and financial data

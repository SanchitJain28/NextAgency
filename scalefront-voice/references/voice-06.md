Transferring from a woocommerce store to shopify is not as simple as importing an CSV file in shopify , it has different phases of migration such as product and collection , blogs and different core pages (Contact Us , about us etc.) , customer accounts and guest account and historical orders . If migration is not done correctly then it can seriosly affect the Recurring revenue and SEO of the store , let's discuss the different phases which are involved in this migration process :

## Different stages of migration

- Product and collection pages : This is the most important and core of the migration , in this phase product and cateogories are imported from woocommerce to shopify using different techniques , if you are small store with 100 to 500 products and each product has less than or equal to 3 options (like color , size etc..) then product and collection import can be done through a simple CSV import . but if you products have complex attributes or have more than 3 options or 2048 variants , then it cannot be done using CSV import as the shopify native CSV importer stripes down options if it is more than 3 , and if one of your product has more than 250 images then it can be a problem because shopify has a hard limit of 250 media images per product , to tackle this we can use product metafeilds , for more complex use cases it is recommended to use a dedicated Shopify app or a GraphQL API middleware to correctly parse the woocommerce products into shopify products

- Blogs and other core pages : to import these pages correctly it is recommended to use GraphQL API or a dedicated shopify apps , Such that it does not loses its structure .

- Customer accounts / Guest accounts : Migrating customer accounts from woocommerce to shopify is not easy because woocommerce stores the user passwords in a dedicated database using bcrypt and on the other hand shopify uses its own auth architecture, hence to import customer account shopify gives an option for customers to enter their emails and they will get a One time password on thier email to verify thier account , NOTE : Import customer accounts before historical orders because shopify binds historical orders with existing customers list in shopify , if it is done later then it can result in empty records and customer will unable to see thier past orders . and before importing customer accounts it is recommended to remove any types of erros in customer emails , make sure you use a middleware which validates the each customer email and then pass it to the shopify customer importer , so that all customers data is migrated safely

- Historical orders : Importing historical orders through shopify CSV is not a good choice because the CSV importer has a hard limt of 15mb on importing historical orders data and orders data can contain other data which cannot be mapped to shopify orders , to tackle this there is several solutions :
  - Use Shopify GraphQL API : This can handle complex orders import and does not have any hard limit , but to make sure to follow the rate limit of GraphQL API , do not use the REST API because this is depricated and can fail.
  - Use a dedicated Shopify App : If you do not have a developer , then it is recommended to use Shopify Historical order import app

- SEO : I consider this stage more important thena product and collection pages because , if not done correctly , it can turn catostrophic for store organic search results and traffic , primarly woocommerce does not have any fixed url architecture like shopify , FYI shopify follows a fixed url architecture for its core pages , for Products : /products/{slug} , for collections : /collections/{slug} , for blogs : /blogs/{slug} and for other pages , /pages/contact-us etc. , if your woocommerce store has a similar urls architecture then the traffic will not be much affected but if he urls structure is different , then it is recommended to add 301 / 308 permanent redirect to old pages which redirect them to new pages , It is also important to export your website GSC data of last 12 months , this will help the developer to properly analyze the top traffic pages and map it to correct urls in shopify such that your store does not lose the traffic

## Short checklist for the migration

- Phase 1 : Export your GSC data of last 12 months , database export via PHP my admin or a dedicated woocommerce plugin , analyze which products have more than 3 options , 250 images and 2048 variants
- Phase 2 : Core products and collection migration , blogs and other pages migration with different checks and tests
- Phase 3. : customer data and historical orders migration
- Phase 4 : SEO migration and mapping to new urls , adding permanent redirect to old urls
- Phase 5 : post launch : make sure sitemap , robots.txt and other SEO core pages are available , and resubmit the sitemap in google search console

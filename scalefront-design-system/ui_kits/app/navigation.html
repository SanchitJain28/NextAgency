Sticky Glassmorphism Navbar

The navbar uses position: sticky; top: 0 with a translucent background (color-mix(in srgb, var(--bg) 88%, transparent)) and backdrop-filter: blur(20px) for the glassmorphism effect. On scroll past 10px, the background opacity increases.

The logo is composed of a 32×32px accent-colored square with the initial "S" plus the "ScaleFront" wordmark in Inter 600 at 22px with -0.02em tracking. Nav links are 15px/500 with 32px gap. The CTA button is a compact 40px-tall primary button.

Scroll this page to see the sticky behavior. The navbar remains fixed at the top.

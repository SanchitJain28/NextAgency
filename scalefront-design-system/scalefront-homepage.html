FAQ

You ask, we answer

How long does a typical build take?
Most projects ship in 4–8 weeks depending on scope. A theme refresh can be done in 2–3 weeks; a full headless rebuild typically takes 6–10 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline after the discovery audit.
Do you work with existing themes?
Yes — we can extend, optimize, or refactor your existing theme. We also build custom themes from scratch when the current foundation is limiting performance or scalability.
What's your engagement model?
Fixed-scope projects for builds and migrations; monthly retainers for ongoing work. Every engagement starts with a paid discovery sprint so both sides align on scope before committing.
Do you handle design or just development?
We're engineering-first but partner with vetted UX designers when you need design. If you already have Figma files, we build pixel-perfect against them — no "design interpretation" required.
What's the minimum project size?
We typically engage with brands doing $1M+ in annual GMV. Our minimum engagement is a two-week discovery sprint at a flat rate, which gives both sides a clear scope before committing to anything larger.
Do you offer post-launch support?
Every build includes a 30-day optimization window. After that, we offer monthly retainer packages with guaranteed response times, proactive monitoring, and sprint-based iteration.

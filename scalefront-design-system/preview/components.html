FAQ Accordion

How long does a typical build take?
Most projects ship in 4–8 weeks depending on scope. A theme refresh can be done in 2–3 weeks; a full headless rebuild typically takes 6–10 weeks.
Do you work with existing themes?
Yes — we can extend, optimize, or refactor your existing theme. We also build custom themes from scratch when the current foundation is limiting performance or scalability.
What's your engagement model?
Fixed-scope projects for builds and migrations; monthly retainers for ongoing work. Every engagement starts with a paid discovery sprint.
